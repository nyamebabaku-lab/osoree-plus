Ɔsɔreɛ Plus

Church projection software that runs from a phone or a computer. It puts Bible verses, song lyrics, pictures, videos, documents and live camera on the church screen, works offline, and is sold per device by Brands Media, Ghana.

The problem it solves: most Ghanaian churches cannot afford a projection laptop, paid software and a trained operator. With Ɔsɔreɛ Plus, one phone connected to a TV or projector runs the whole service, with no internet needed during the service. When the church wants more, it can stream live, add Wi-Fi screens, and let the app catch scripture references as the preacher speaks.

Who it is for

  • Churches of every size, from house fellowships to big congregations, in Ghana first and then abroad.
  • The operator (often a young volunteer) who runs the screen from a phone, tablet or laptop.
  • Brands Media (Super Admin), who sells licences, approves payments, runs the store and publishes updates from the admin dashboard.

What it does

Bible Free

KJV, ASV, BSB, WEB, YLT and BBE built in. Fast search, shows several verses together, compares two versions side by side, voice search.

KJV+ Strong's Pro

KJV with tap-to-read Greek and Hebrew word meanings. Included in the free trial.

Songs Free

Type or paste lyrics; the app splits them into verse and chorus slides automatically.

Pictures and videos Free

Backgrounds, announcements and videos with loop, sound and volume controls.

Service plan Free

Line up the order of service ahead of time and step through it with Next and Previous.

Documents Pro

PDF, PowerPoint, Word and Excel files turned into slides on the device.

Live video Pro

Camera on screen with lower thirds, name tags, scrolling ticker and picture-in-picture.

Live streaming Pro

Stream to YouTube, Facebook, TikTok and others. Stream keys stay on the device.

Wi-Fi screens Pro

Extra screens and a second phone used as a Wi-Fi camera, all over the church Wi-Fi.

Scripture listener Pro

Listens to the preacher, spots references such as "John 3:16" and shows a card with the verse. The phone buzzes and the operator taps Show; nothing goes on screen without the operator.

Logo watermark Pro

The church's own logo on the screen and stream.

Backup and restore Pro

Save songs, plans and settings and move them to another device.

How it reaches the screen

  • Android: HDMI cable or adapter, or screen casting, to a TV or projector. The phone shows the controls; the screen shows only the output.
  • Windows: the output opens full-screen on the second monitor or projector.
  • Wi-Fi screens: any TV browser or device on the same Wi-Fi can show the output (Pro).

How churches pay

  1. Free trial. Every new device gets every Pro feature for the trial period (currently 15 days, set by the admin).
  2. After the trial. The free features keep working, but a "Free version" mark appears on the screen and everywhere else. The admin can also block expired trials completely. A blocked device shows Get Pro, Update and contact buttons (e-mail and WhatsApp), and it unlocks by itself once Pro is active.
  3. Buying Pro. Either "Pro for this device" (no end date) or "Pro for one year". In Ghana: Mobile Money or card, in cedis. Outside Ghana: card only, in US dollars. The country is detected from the internet connection and the SIM card. Churches can also pay by MoMo directly and send a request with the transaction ID for the admin to approve.
  4. App stores. On iPhone, purchases go through Apple in-app purchase; in the Google Play version, through Google Play billing. Both are checked against Apple's and Google's signatures.
  5. Licences. Each licence is digitally signed and tied to one device code (for example OSR-7K2M-9QXP-4D8A). Pro can be moved to a new phone with an SMS code.

Prices are set in the admin dashboard and can change at any time. No refunds once a licence or item is activated, except for payment errors.

The store

The admin uploads extra Bible translations (only those Brands Media is licensed to sell) and picture or short-video backgrounds, free or paid. A purchase belongs to the church's phone number, so every device the church links gets the item, and it installs automatically. Each item can have a Ghana price, a US dollar price and App Store / Play product IDs.

The admin dashboard

Home

Revenue in cedis and dollars, active devices, and a reminder to block devices whose trial has ended.

Requests

Approve or decline MoMo Pro and store requests; approval reaches the device straight away.

Devices

Every install with model, system, country, version and feature use. Block, unblock and filter.

Updates

Publish a new version as optional or required with a "What's new" note. Apps show "A new version is available" and can update automatically.

Licences and codes

Issue, revoke and move licences; create prepaid codes for dealers.

Settings

Trial length, Pro features, Ghana and US dollar prices, MoMo details, support contacts, test mode.

Privacy, rights and trust

  • Bibles, songs, media, documents and everything projected stay on the device. The server receives only the device code, app and device details, country, feature counts and purchase records.
  • No adverts, no selling of data, no GPS. The scripture listener uses the phone's own speech engine; Brands Media never receives audio or text.
  • Built-in Bibles are public domain or openly licensed. Churches are responsible for song copyright (for example a CCLI licence).
  • Published Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions and copyright notices, written to Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

How it is built

  • One shared code base (React) packaged for Android and iPhone (Capacitor) and Windows (Electron), plus the web.
  • Server on Supabase: database, file storage and one server function for licences, payments, the store, devices and updates.
  • Payments: Paystack or Hubtel, Apple in-app purchase and Google Play billing. SMS: mNotify. Country lookup: ipinfo.io.
  • 420 automated checks cover the app, licences, payments, the store, the listener, updates and the admin dashboard; all pass on 1.10.1.
