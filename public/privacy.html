Ɔsɔreɛ Plus Privacy Policy

Applies to Ɔsɔreɛ Plus for Android, iPhone, Windows and the web, and the Ɔsɔreɛ Admin app. Last updated: 18 November 2026 (country pricing).

In short: Ɔsɔreɛ Plus works on your device. Your Bibles, songs, pictures, videos, documents, sermon notes and what you put on the screen stay on your device and are not sent to us. We only receive what is needed to run your free trial and Pro licence, to process purchases, to send you updates, and to keep the service safe. We do not show adverts, we do not sell information, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who we are

Ɔsɔreɛ Plus is provided by its owner trading as Brands Media, Ghana ("we", "us"), the data controller. Contact: nyamebabaku@gmail.com. We are responsible for the information described in this policy.

What stays on your device

  • Bibles (built-in, imported or downloaded from the store), songs, plans, announcements and settings.
  • Pictures, videos, PDF, PowerPoint, Word and Excel files you add. Documents are turned into pages on your device.
  • Camera and microphone: the live camera picture is shown on your projector, Wi‑Fi screens and, if you choose to go live, on the streaming platforms you set up. Stream keys stay on your device.
  • Backups you make are saved where you choose.

Purchases

  • Mobile Money or card (website/APK/Windows version): payments are handled by Paystack or Hubtel; card details are entered on their secure pages and never reach us. Outside Ghana only card payment is offered, in US dollars (or in Ghana cedis at the rate shown). If you pay us directly by MoMo, you send us the transaction ID so we can approve your request.
  • iPhone (App Store): purchases are made with your Apple ID through Apple's in-app purchase. Apple tells us the purchase is genuine (a signed record containing the product, date and a transaction number). We do not receive your Apple ID, name or payment details. "Restore purchases" uses the same records.
  • Google Play: purchases are made through Google Play billing. Google confirms the purchase to us (product, order number, date and state). We do not receive your Google account details or payment method.
  • Refunds for App Store and Google Play purchases are handled by Apple and Google. A refunded purchase stops the matching licence or store item.

Features that use outside services

  • Scripture listener and voice search: speech is turned into text by your phone's own speech engine (Apple Speech on iPhone, Google speech services on Android). On most newer phones this happens on the phone itself; on some phones the phone's maker processes the audio under their own privacy policy. The app only looks for Bible references in the text. We never receive the audio or the text.
  • SMS codes (moving Pro to a new phone, linking a church's phones) are sent by mNotify to the phone number you enter.
  • Notifications (scripture heard, Pro approved, item installed) are created on your device. You can turn them off in your phone's settings.
  • Live streaming sends your camera picture and sound to the platforms you choose (YouTube, Facebook, TikTok and others), under their policies.

Who processes information for us

  • Supabase (hosting and database, servers in the European Union) stores the information listed above.
  • ipinfo.io tells us the country of an internet address (the address is sent, only the country comes back).
  • GitHub hosts the website and app downloads.
  • Paystack, Hubtel, Apple, Google: payments; mNotify: SMS.

They may only use the information to provide their service to us. We do not sell or rent information to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Information sent outside Ghana

Our servers and some of the services above are outside Ghana (mainly in the European Union and the United States). We only use providers that protect information with security measures at least as strong as those required by Ghana's Data Protection Act, and we send them only what they need.

Updates and blocking

The app checks for new versions about once a day. If you turn on "Update automatically", new versions download by themselves (on Android you still confirm the install). We may block a device that breaks our terms, for example by misusing a licence or not paying for one. A blocked device stops working until it is unblocked. Contact us if you think a device was blocked by mistake.

How long we keep information

  • Device records: while the app is in use, and up to 2 years after the device last checks in.
  • Licences and payment records: as long as the licence is valid and as long as tax and accounting law requires (usually 6 years).
  • SMS codes: deleted when used or after 10 minutes. The SMS delivery log: 12 months.

Your choices and rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), and similar laws where you live, you may ask us what information we hold about your device or church, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You may also object to how we use it or ask for a copy. Write to nyamebabaku@gmail.com with your device code (shown in the app under Screen → Pro) or the phone number used for purchases. Deleting your information also removes any Pro licence or store purchases linked to it, unless the law requires us to keep the payment record. You can also stop all information going to us by not connecting the app to the internet: everything except buying, updates and restoring works offline. If you are not happy with our answer, you may complain to Ghana's Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.org.gh) or the authority in your country.

Children

Ɔsɔreɛ Plus is made for churches and is operated by adults. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

  • Everything travels encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Card and MoMo details are handled only by the payment providers.
  • Licences are digitally signed, and App Store / Google Play purchases are checked against Apple's and Google's signatures, so they cannot be faked.
  • The database is not reachable by the public: every table is locked and only our server can read it. Store files are private and given out only through short-lived links to devices that own them.
  • The admin dashboard needs a confirmed e-mail and password and is limited to the Super Admin.
  • We keep the information we hold to the minimum described here and delete it on the schedule below. If a breach affecting your information ever happens, we will tell you and the Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
